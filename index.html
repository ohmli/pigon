How it works: an object detector (COCO-SSD) looks for animal-shaped things, then an image classifier (MobileNet, trained on ImageNet's “fox squirrel” class) checks each one, plus the full frame. It works best with a clear, reasonably large squirrel. Dark footage, tiny or partly hidden squirrels, and chipmunks may be missed, and cats or branches occasionally fool it; raise the confidence slider if you get false alarms.
